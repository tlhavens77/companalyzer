Look-up fills the fields below. Anything missing or wrong, just type it in.

Cost-approach inputs (used only when fewer than 3 sold comps are found)

Estimate = (sq ft × cost per sq ft) − depreciation (age × rate, capped at 60%) + land/extras. Land value fills in from the county's tax assessment when the lookup finds one. Assessments are often a percentage of market value, so set the ratio to match your county (100 if it assesses at full value) and check the figure. Cost per sq ft starts from the published state average when the lookup finds the property's state. Type your own figure to replace it; a figure you type is remembered on this device.

Sold prices are editable. If a comp looks wrong, fix its price or clear the row, and the result updates.
